# The Challenge of the Transition to AGI**El desafío de la transición a la AGI**Preprint by **Miguel Ángel de la Osa Cabezas**, published in Spanish and English.This article argues that the decisive risk posed by artificial general intelligence lies not only in the final state of a possible superintelligence, but in the transition toward it. Against the false choice between absolute control and naïve trust, it proposes preserving an interval between capacity and action: an institutional and cognitive space in which information can be compared, objections can arise, judgment can remain revisable, and reversible decisions can be preferred.## Read version 2.0- [English — *The Challenge of the Transition to AGI*](de-la-osa-cabezas-2026-challenge-transition-agi-en-v2.0.pdf)- [Español — *El desafío de la transición a la AGI*](de-la-osa-cabezas-2026-desafio-transicion-agi-es-v2.0.pdf)
